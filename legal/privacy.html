1. What this covers

This policy explains what information Dr Frigz collects through learn.drfrigz.com, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

2. Information we collect

CategoryExamples
Account informationName, email address, username, profile details you add

3. How we use this information

  • To provide and maintain your account and course access
  • To process payments for paid courses (via Stripe)
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset, course updates)
  • To maintain platform security and prevent abuse

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the platform:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (verification, password reset, notifications)
  • Our infrastructure/hosting provider, to run the platform itself

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies

This platform uses cookies necessary for authentication and session management (e.g. keeping you signed in, remembering your organization context). These are functional cookies required for the platform to work, not third-party advertising trackers.

6. Data retention

We retain account and course data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access controls) to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

9. Children's privacy

This platform is intended for professional training use and is not directed at children under 16.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
